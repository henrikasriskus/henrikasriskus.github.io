Intro

Established in 2020. Short-term annual artist residency in Northern Portugal. A collaborative, community-driven space.

01. Arrival

24km from Porto. Bus line from Gaia. Arrive on the first scheduled day, or arrange other dates.

02. Facilities

Workshop with natural light. Equipped for iron, wood, ceramics. Video and audio gear available.

03. Stay

Apply early for limited vacant rooms. Tents and camper vans can be set up at the camping area.

04. Climate

Rough! Really warm and cold in 24 hours. Bring both summer clothes and warm garments. Pool and river nearby.

05. Cost

Collaborative effort. Bar/food covers expenses. Two main meals per day. Conscious contribution requested.
